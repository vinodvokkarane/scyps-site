Ryan McCann

Ph.D. Candidate, joined 2024. Advised by Vinod M. Vokkarane.

Co-founder and lead developer of FUSION (github.com/SDNNetSim/FUSION), supported by MIT I-Corps and AT&T; reinforcement learning for software-defined elastic optical networks; failure-aware routing and realistic simulation of elastic optical and mesh networks.
