From the authors of the lab\'s related work in the project period.

Architecture

What the work found

  • Cyber Security Constrained Economic Dispatch for Resilient Power System Operation (2025): Dispatch that stays secure under cyber contingencies.
  • Disaster-Resilient Cyber-Physical Distribution System Reconfiguration and Dynamic Networked Microgrid Formation under Intermittent Generation (2025): Faster recovery with intermittent generation.
  • Exploring a Smart FDI Attack and Enhancing Anomaly Detection in Smart Meters (2025): Detection that catches attacks existing methods miss.
  • Observability-Aware Resilient PMU Networking (2024): Fast, resilient PMU data transfer.
