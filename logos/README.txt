Official sponsor and partner logos go in this folder. The build script embeds any
file it finds here, named by key, and shows a typeset name for the rest.

Accepted formats: .svg (preferred), .png, .jpg. Use the full-color version on a
transparent or white background, at least 400 px wide.

  nsf.svg        U.S. National Science Foundation  (required on NSF-funded pages;
                 files and rules at https://www.nsf.gov/policies/brand)
  doe.svg        U.S. Department of Energy
  onr.svg        Office of Naval Research
  army.svg       U.S. Army
  airforce.svg   U.S. Air Force
  justice.svg    National Institute of Justice (Dept. of Justice)
  usmc.svg       U.S. Marine Corps
